🎯 SIH Problem Statement

Weather conditions can change rapidly and may create risks for citizens, travellers, farmers and local communities. Traditional weather information may not provide a simple, location-focused risk view.

💡 Our Solution

SIH Weather Guard is a smart weather monitoring prototype that converts live weather data into an easy-to-understand risk level: LOW, MODERATE or HIGH.

The system combines current weather information, forecasts, multi-city monitoring, maps, analytics and smart alerts in one dashboard.

⚙️ How It Works

1️⃣ Location

User searches a city or uses current location.

2️⃣ Weather Data

Live weather information is retrieved from the weather API.

3️⃣ Risk Analysis

Temperature, wind, rain and weather conditions are analysed.

4️⃣ Alert

The dashboard highlights the risk and provides a recommendation.
